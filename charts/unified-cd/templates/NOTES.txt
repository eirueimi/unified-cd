unified-cd was installed with the bundled PostgreSQL and Garage and fixed
development credentials. Use it to try the product, not to run real work.

Reach the controller from your machine:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/unified-cd-controller 8080:{{ .Values.controller.service.port }}

Then open http://localhost:8080 and sign in with the admin token:

  dev-token-change-me

To move to production, set bundledStores.enabled=false, create your own
Secret and pass controller.existingSecret (see the chart README), put HTTPS in
front of the controller, and give the controller a KEK Secret
({{ .Values.controller.kek.secretName }}).
